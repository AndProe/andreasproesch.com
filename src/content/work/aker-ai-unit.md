title: How we built the Aker AI Unit
description: Setting up and running the task force that took Aker's industrial portfolio from first generative-AI experiments to working industrial AI use cases.
org: Aker ASA
date: 2023-01-01
---

In early 2023, ChatGPT was only a few months old, and it was mostly used to write poems and not-so-funny wedding speeches. Inside Aker, we were asking a different question: what could happen when this tech meets an offshore platform, an engineering office, or an energy company?

Aker was a natural place to ask it. The group owns many of Norway's leading industrial companies across energy, offshore, and engineering, and it has a habit of moving early on technology. Years before, it had founded Cognite on the bet that industrial data would transform heavy industry. Now a new wave was here, and it was obvious to us that the generative AI wave would hit hard — though it also could provide the opportunity for the surf of a lifetime.

Aker chose to surf, and I was asked to make it happen.

The assignment was to set up and run the Aker AI Unit, a task force that brought together the CDOs and CTOs from across the portfolio. On paper the mandate was simple: map where this technology could create value, get the first use cases working in practice, and upskill our organizations so they could lead on industrial AI in their own fields.

It was 2023; there was no playbook. The technology was changing month by month, and every company in the portfolio was facing the same questions at the same time. That turned out to be our advantage. Instead of each company working it out alone, we worked it out together, sharing what worked, what didn't, and what we were learning along the way.

We didn't want to produce a strategy deck and call it a day. We wanted real solutions in the hands of real people, so we built them. Some of those early solutions grew into what are today among the leading industrial AI use cases.

Looking back, what I am most proud of is the foundation. The Aker AI Unit gave a group of very different industrial companies a shared starting point for their AI journey, and that journey is still going.
