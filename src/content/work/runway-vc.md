Aker is one of Norway's leading industrial groups, with companies across energy, offshore, and engineering and a history going back more than 180 years. That history gives it a practical view of which technologies heavy industry will adopt and which it will not.

Aker helped launch RunwayVC to put that view to work for startups. The fund backs early-stage industrial technology companies and gives them direct access to industrial companies that can test and buy their products, which most investors cannot offer.

I have worked with industrial companies and startups for my whole career. For the past several years I have worked closely with the RunwayVC team, helping their portfolio companies develop and assessing new investment opportunities.

Much of that work is tracking what frontier startups are building and judging which of it will hold up in real operations. Current focus is the next generation of industrial technology, particularly physical AI: AI built into machines, robots, and industrial plants.
